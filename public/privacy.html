Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Overview

CodePad+ is a local plain-text editor for Android. Notes are stored on the user's device. The app does not require an account or cloud synchronization.

Notes and files

Notes created or imported into CodePad+ are stored locally in the app's internal storage. When you use Save As, Backup All, Restore Backup, Export All, Share, or Open With, Android's system file or sharing interfaces may be used to access files you select.

Data collection

The current version of CodePad+ does not intentionally collect, transmit, or sell the contents of your notes.

Internet access and third-party services

Support links such as Website, Feedback, Donate, Contact, or Google Play may open external apps or websites. Those services may have their own privacy policies.

Backups

CodePad+ does not currently provide cloud backup. Users are responsible for creating and safely storing backups of important notes.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact you@example.com.
